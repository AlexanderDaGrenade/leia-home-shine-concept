    Ask us a question
    What does a cleaning visit cost?

    Pricing depends on the size of your home, the type of clean, and how much time it takes. We'll give you a clear, free estimate before any work starts, with no hidden fees.

    How do I book a cleaning?

    Call us at (973) 787-6266 or fill in the estimate form on this page. We'll confirm your quote and find a time that works for you.

    Can I get an estimate before I commit?

    Yes. Every estimate is free and comes with no obligation, so you'll know exactly what's included and what it costs before booking.

    What's the best cleaning plan for me?

    Most busy households choose weekly or bi-weekly recurring visits. If it's been a while, we usually recommend starting with a deep clean. Tell us about your home and we'll suggest the right fit.

    Are there ways to save on cleaning?

    New clients get 10% off their first cleaning. Ask about recurring plans when you request your estimate.

    What if I need to change or cancel my cleaning?

    Plans change, and we understand. Just let us know as early as you can and we'll work with you to reschedule.
